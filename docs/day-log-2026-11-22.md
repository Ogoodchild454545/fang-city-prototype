## v0.15.0 — commit 4c606a3 (04:25 UK) — battle UX from the best tactics games
- Wrote `docs/best-of-turn-based.md`: what XCOM 2, Into the Breach, Midnight Suns, Mario + Rabbids, Fire Emblem,
  Darkest Dungeon, Demeo, Hard West and Invisible Inc do best, and which ideas fit a 3–5 minute phone op.
- Move preview on touch: tapping a tile shows the path, the AP cost, cover there and which rivals could shoot you
  (red lines with their %). Tap "Move here" to go. Mouse users get the same preview on hover.
- Undo move: a plain move that set nothing off can be taken back.
- Attacking is clearer: tapping a rival shows one big button, e.g. "Shoot 70% · 2–4 dmg", coloured by the odds.
  Mouse click on a rival in range fires straight away; right-click shows their details.
- Tapping a rival shows the tiles it can reach this turn in red (danger zone).
- Battle speed button 1× / 2× (remembered).
- The camera follows a moving piece.
- End turn pulses and says "0 AP" when the operator is out of actions.
- First-time hints on the board; ability tips no longer get stuck on phones.
- Faster drawing: movement range is worked out once per board change instead of every frame.

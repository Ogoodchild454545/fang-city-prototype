## v0.19.1 — Tutorial hints and deselect

From Oliver's feedback on the first mission: the "Rook is a Bruiser, he wins close up" pop-up read badly and hid
the board; "Pounce reaches 4 tiles" showed after the AP was already spent; there was no way to deselect.

- New `js/ui/tutor.js`: an ordered tutorial-step runner (`{id, when, done?, html, once?}`), learned per device
  under `fangcity.hints`. Battle steps: turn/AP → move → cover/threat preview → ability (only if affordable, off
  cooldown and a target in range) → target → odds → end turn → deselect/inspect. The first-mission coach is a
  state-aware fallback after the steps are learned. The guided intro (told through Rook) will reuse it.
- Hints are one compact tap-to-dismiss toast at a clear edge (top-right, top-left or bottom-right), placed away
  from the acting unit, the inspected unit, Hook and the preview tile; hidden if no edge is clear.
- Deselect: tap self, tap empty ground, or the ✕ on the unit bar. Deselected: slim "X is up · tap to select" bar,
  no range or action bar, tapping any piece shows its status panel; a blue-tile tap reselects and previews.
- Tests: unit (72 pass), `tests/v0191.py`, and the full Playwright suites at 844×390 and 844×340 (playthrough
  and v018 run alone) all pass.
